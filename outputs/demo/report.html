⚠️ Operational Caveats & Critical Limitations

  • Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Physics: Sentinel-1 C-band SAR is sensitive to smooth standing water, but exhibits limitations in dense urban zones (where building corners cause strong double-bounce reflections that mask water) and under dense forest canopies. Conversely, smooth tarmac (runways, paved highways) can mimic water backscatter.
  • Optical Limitations: Sentinel-2 optical verification is blocked by persistent cloud cover, typical during peak monsoon flooding.
  • Temporal Snapshot & Peak Flood Gap: The pre/post acquisition gap (48 days) captures an instantaneous satellite pass; it does not necessarily coincide with the peak crest of the flood wave.
  • Estimated Exposure Figures: "People affected" figures are statistical approximations calculated by intersecting gridded WorldPop estimates with flooded surface fractions; they are not direct headcounts or verified field counts.
  • Infrastructure Network Completeness: Road cuts and critical facility hit counts are subject to OpenStreetMap data completeness in this AOI. Unmapped rural roads and clinics are not reflected in these scores.
  • Decision Protocol: Zones marked as VERIFY exhibit high compound risk scores but low detection confidence; response teams should deploy ground scouts or drone reconnaissance before dispatching heavy equipment.
